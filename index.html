About

About Anytime Telecare

A nurse-led clinical support service providing after-hours telephone triage for healthcare practices across the country.

Anytime Telecare is a nurse-led clinical support service that provides after-hours telephone triage for healthcare practices. The organization partners with pediatric and primary care providers to ensure patients have access to timely clinical guidance when offices are closed.


Using standardized protocols, experienced registered nurses assess patient symptoms, provide care recommendations, and guide patients to the most appropriate level of care. When needed, calls are escalated to on-call providers, and detailed encounter summaries are shared with the patient's primary practice to support continuity of care.

24/7 Clinical Access

Through around-the-clock access to clinical support, Anytime Telecare helps practices extend care beyond regular hours and improve patient access.

Reducing Unnecessary Utilization

By guiding patients to the right level of care, the service reduces unnecessary emergency department and urgent care utilization.

Overview

Project Overview

This project evaluates the impact of after-hours nurse-led telephone triage on healthcare utilization, with a focus on reducing avoidable emergency department and urgent care visits. A significant portion of ED use is driven by low-acuity conditions, particularly during evenings and weekends when access to primary care is limited.

Data Analysis

Examination of de-identified triage encounter data to understand call patterns, patient needs, and care outcomes, including disposition categories and escalation rates.

Literature Review

Review of national studies and policy sources (e.g., CMS, AHRQ) to benchmark ED avoidance, cost differences, and the effectiveness of tele-triage models.

Qualitative Insights

Interviews with providers and stakeholders to better understand care pathways, patient behavior, and real-world implementation challenges.

Integrated Findings

Together, these methods provide a comprehensive view of how after-hours triage influences patient decision-making, supports continuity of care, and reduces reliance on high-cost emergency services.

Key Findings

Analysis of Anytime Telecare's triage data shows that the majority of patient concerns are low-acuity and can be safely managed outside high-cost care settings. Utilization is heavily concentrated during after-hours periods.

Qualitative Insight

Interviews with providers and practice staff indicate that patients experience minimal wait times and high reassurance from triage support. Providers also reported improved workflow efficiency and reduced after-hours burden, particularly for non-urgent cases.

Care Outcomes Following Triage

The majority of cases are managed through lower-intensity care pathways, with only 7.46% resulting in emergency department referrals, indicating minimal escalation to high-cost care.

When Patients Seek Care

Strategy

Recommendations

Based on the findings, several strategies can strengthen the value and scalability of Anytime Telecare while enhancing its impact on reducing unnecessary ED utilization.

01

Strengthen Payer-Facing Value & ROI Modeling

Develop more refined, payer-specific economic models that incorporate reimbursement rates, net savings, and value-based care metrics to improve credibility and support adoption among risk-bearing organizations.

02

Integrate Claims Data to Validate Outcomes

Linking triage encounters with actual utilization data can enable direct measurement of ED and urgent care visits following triage, strengthening evidence of care diversion beyond modeled estimates.

03

Focus on High-Impact Populations

Given that the majority of utilization comes from pediatric patients, targeted strategies such as caregiver education and condition-specific pathways can further improve triage effectiveness for common low-acuity conditions.

04

Increase Patient Awareness & Utilization

Expanding awareness through patient education and integrating triage access into routine care touchpoints (e.g., patient portals, reminders) can reduce unnecessary ED visits by encouraging patients to seek guidance first.

05

Align with Value-Based Care Models

Positioning Anytime Telecare as a tool for improving care coordination, access, and cost efficiency can support adoption among accountable care organizations and managed care plans.

06

Quantify Provider Experience Benefits

Measuring reductions in after-hours workload and improvements in provider well-being can strengthen the value proposition beyond financial savings.

07

Invest in Data Infrastructure & Future Research

Enhancing analytics capabilities, integrating with health systems, and conducting prospective studies can improve transparency and provide stronger evidence for long-term impact.

Together, these recommendations position Anytime Telecare as a data-driven, scalable solution capable of improving care access, reducing unnecessary utilization, and supporting value-based healthcare delivery.
